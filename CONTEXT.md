# Consorcio Fenix Sun Exposure

This context describes route, location, and sun-exposure concepts for advising onboard bus passengers where direct sun is likely to enter the bus.

## Language

**Sun Exposure**:
Direct sunlight entering the bus cabin along a route segment for a given travel direction and datetime.
_Avoid_: Heat, temperature prediction, burning

**Geometric Sun Exposure**:
Sun exposure estimated from route geometry and sun position, assuming an unobstructed sky.
_Avoid_: Shadow-aware exposure, weather-adjusted exposure

**Sun-side Advisory**:
A passenger-facing recommendation about which side or area of the bus is likely exposed to direct sunlight.
_Avoid_: Heat map, thermal forecast

**Trip Exposure Summary**:
An aggregate of exposure directions across a scheduled trip, weighted by distance or estimated travel time.
_Avoid_: Single segment result, temperature summary

**Upcoming Exposure Window**:
The near-term portion of an onboard trip, defaulting to the next 15 minutes from the passenger's current position.
_Avoid_: Whole trip, current segment only

**Remaining Route Exposure**:
The sun exposure from the passenger's current projected route segment through the end of the selected route direction.
_Avoid_: Upcoming window, full route from origin

**Projected Route Position**:
The passenger's current location snapped to the nearest materialized route segment of the selected route direction.
_Avoid_: Raw GPS point, stop location

**Onboard Advisory**:
A sun-side advisory for a passenger who is already on the bus, based on current location and datetime.
_Avoid_: Pre-trip schedule advisory, departure advisory

**Nearby Route Discovery**:
Finding route directions whose geometry passes within a radius of a user's location.
_Avoid_: Stop search, route recommendation

**Candidate Route Direction**:
A nearby route direction presented to an onboard passenger for selection.
_Avoid_: Automatically selected route, confirmed route

**Candidate Direction Label**:
A passenger-facing route-direction label based on source service labels such as "Saída Bairro" or "Saída TICEN".
_Avoid_: Raw KML label, technical direction name

**Advisory Confidence**:
The confidence that a sun-side advisory uses the intended directed route geometry and appropriate timing assumptions.
_Avoid_: Accuracy guarantee, weather confidence

**Passenger-facing Side**:
The left or right side of the bus from the perspective of a passenger facing the direction of travel.
_Avoid_: Map side, inbound side, outbound side

**Exposure Direction**:
The passenger-facing area where direct sun is likely to enter the bus: left, right, front, back, overhead, or none.
_Avoid_: Map direction, compass direction

**Sun Position**:
The sun azimuth and elevation for a route location at a timezone-aware datetime.
_Avoid_: Weather, sunlight intensity

**Segment Timestamp Estimate**:
An estimated datetime for when a bus reaches a route segment during a scheduled trip.
_Avoid_: Live position, exact arrival time

**Nominal Trip Duration**:
A configured or inferred trip duration used when the source provides only departure times.
_Avoid_: Scheduled arrival time, actual travel time

**Nominal Bus Speed**:
A global configured speed used to estimate trip progress when no travel-time source exists.
_Avoid_: Real speed, scheduled speed

**Route Direction**:
A directed route geometry whose coordinate order represents bus movement from start to end.
_Avoid_: Route shape, path

**Route Direction Kind**:
A nullable source-label classification of a Route Direction as `ida` or `volta`, assigned only to an unambiguous pair within the same Route Version.
_Avoid_: Service Direction, inferred complementary direction

**Route Segment**:
A short directed portion of a route direction used as the unit for sun exposure evaluation.
_Avoid_: Stop leg, whole route

**Materialized Route Segment**:
A persisted route segment with precomputed geometry, sequence, bearing, distance, cumulative distance, and source-segment lineage; source segments longer than 250 meters are split.
_Avoid_: Query-time segment, virtual segment

**Service Direction**:
A schedule-page departure group that may resolve to one route direction.
_Avoid_: Direction, trip direction

## Relationships

- A **Service Direction** may resolve to one **Route Direction**.
- A **Route Direction Kind** is assigned only when a **Route Version** contains exactly two Route Directions: one whose source name contains only the whole word `ida` and one whose source name contains only the whole word `volta`.
- A **Route Direction** contains ordered **Materialized Route Segments**.
- A **Materialized Route Segment** belongs to exactly one **Route Direction**.
- **Materialized Route Segments** are created during route ingestion and may be regenerated by a rebuild command when the segmentation algorithm changes.
- **Materialized Route Segment** sequence must preserve the source **Route Direction** coordinate order.
- **Sun Exposure** is evaluated for one **Route Direction** at the passenger's current datetime for onboard use.
- **Sun Exposure** depends on **Sun Position** at each segment midpoint and the bearing of each **Materialized Route Segment**.
- The advisory app should compute **Sun Position** with Astral for the first version.
- **Geometric Sun Exposure** does not account for buildings, trees, terrain, clouds, curtains, or vehicle-specific shading.
- A **Segment Timestamp Estimate** may be derived from **Nominal Bus Speed** for secondary pre-trip flows when no arrival time is available.
- A **Sun-side Advisory** is derived from **Sun Exposure**.
- A **Sun-side Advisory** names an **Exposure Direction**.
- **Exposure Direction** uses passenger-facing orientation: positive sun azimuth delta from segment bearing means right side, negative means left side.
- **Exposure Direction** is `none` when the sun is below the horizon and `overhead` when sun elevation is at least 70 degrees.
- A **Sun-side Advisory** does not model seat rows or fleet-specific cabin layouts in the first version.
- A **Trip Exposure Summary** is derived from segment-level **Sun Exposure**.
- A pre-trip **Sun-side Advisory** is shown by default only when service-to-route-direction match confidence is medium or high; low-confidence matches are reserved for debug or audit workflows.
- An **Onboard Advisory** can trust a passenger-selected **Candidate Route Direction** without relying on service-direction match confidence.
- **Nearby Route Discovery** uses **Materialized Route Segment** proximity, not stop proximity, until reliable stop data exists.
- An **Onboard Advisory** starts from the passenger's current location, not a scheduled departure time.
- **Nearby Route Discovery** produces **Candidate Route Directions**; the passenger selects the route direction before an **Onboard Advisory** is trusted.
- A **Candidate Route Direction** should use a **Candidate Direction Label** when a service label is available, falling back to the raw route-direction name only when necessary.
- A **Candidate Route Direction** requires materialized segments; a service label improves display but does not determine eligibility.
- An **Onboard Advisory** prioritizes the **Upcoming Exposure Window** and may also include **Remaining Route Exposure**.
- An **Onboard Advisory** uses a **Projected Route Position** and should be withheld when the projection exceeds the configured off-route threshold, defaulting to 75 meters.
- Public **Nearby Route Discovery** and **Onboard Advisory** use only current routes and current route versions.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should we predict actual cabin temperature for this departure?"
> **Domain expert:** "No -- first version should produce a **Sun-side Advisory** from **Sun Exposure**, not a thermal model."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "hotter" and "burning" were narrowed to **Sun Exposure** for the first version; actual temperature prediction is out of scope until a separate thermal model exists.
- The primary use case is an **Onboard Advisory**; scheduled departure times are secondary and are not needed for current-location sun exposure.
- Consorcio Fenix provides departure times but not trip durations; secondary pre-trip **Segment Timestamp Estimates** use **Nominal Bus Speed** rather than scheduled arrival times.
- First-version **Sun-side Advisories** are based on **Geometric Sun Exposure**, not shadow-aware or weather-aware exposure.
